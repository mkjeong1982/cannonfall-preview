Cannonfall Privacy Policy

Cannonfall · App Store (iPhone / iPad) version

1. General

MK Game Studio (the "Operator") operates the mobile game 'Cannonfall' (the "Service") in compliance with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws, and processes only the minimum information needed. There is no sign-up and no account: you play as a guest.

2. Information We Process and Why

a. Stored only on your device - Game progress (waves reached, gems, coins, cannons and looks, upgrades, settings), ad counters and your purchases' state: to keep your progress. The Operator cannot see them. The store app also keeps a copy in the cloud under your account (see the account item below). b. Online matches (when you play online) - A random player identifier made on your device, the name "Guest", your cannon's look and loadout, and match data (moves, results, ranking points): to run matches against other players. c. Anonymous gameplay statistics (automatic) - A random device identifier, a session identifier, game events (e.g., waves started and ended, upgrades, shop and ad results, errors), device class (phone / tablet), OS, app version and time: to see where the game can be better. No name, email, advertising ID or account is included. d. Ads (Google AdMob, with Google's consent tool UMP; Unity Ads when AdMob has no ad) - Google may collect IP address (rough location), device and app information and ad interactions: to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. The Service does not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used. - Unity Ads (Unity Technologies), shown only when AdMob has no ad, may collect IP address (rough location), the vendor identifier (IDFV), device and app information and ad interactions: to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used (no tracking permission is asked). Where the law asks for consent and it isn't given, Unity shows non-personalized ads. e. Purchases (RevenueCat) - An anonymous RevenueCat user identifier, purchased product IDs, purchase and restore history and App Store receipt information: to verify purchases, deliver items and restore them. Card numbers and other payment details are handled by Apple; the Operator does not receive them. f. Account and cloud save (store app, Firebase Authentication / Cloud Firestore) - A random Firebase user ID (you start as a guest) and, if you link an account, your Google or Apple user ID and email address (Apple may give a relay address): to keep your progress in the cloud and bring it back on another device. - Your saved game progress (the items in a.), stored under that user ID: only you can read it. g. App statistics and crash reports (store app, Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics) - An app instance ID, the game events in c., device model, OS, app version, country / region (from the IP address) and crash / error reports: to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes. The advertising ID is not collected for these. h. Contact by email (optional) - Your email address and message: to answer you.

3. Retention and Deletion

a. Data on your device: until you delete the app or use Settings > Reset Data. b. Online match data and ranking points: kept while you play online; a player identifier unused for one year may be deleted. Ask us to delete them at any time (see the data deletion page). c. Anonymous gameplay statistics: kept for up to 3 months, then deleted automatically. d. Purchase records: kept for 5 years under the obligation to keep payment and supply records. The Stores keep the payment ledger. e. Emails: kept for 3 years as complaint-handling records, then deleted. f. Data held by Google (ads) and RevenueCat follows their own retention policies. g. Account and cloud save: until you delete the account (Settings > Account > Delete account), then deleted at once. App statistics (Firebase Analytics): 14 months. Crash reports: 90 days.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

The Operator does not sell or provide your information to third parties, except to the processors below and where required by law.

5. Processors and International Transfers

Your information is sent abroad over the network when you use the related features. a. Cloudflare, Inc. (USA): hosting of the online match server and the anonymous gameplay statistics (Workers, Durable Objects, Workers Analytics Engine) - Items: random player / device identifiers, match data, game events b. Google LLC (USA): AdMob ads and the consent tool (UMP) - Items: as in section 2(d) — under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) c. RevenueCat, Inc. (USA): in-app purchase verification and restoring - Items: as in section 2(e) d. Apple Inc. (USA): App Store payments - Under Apple's privacy policy. e. Google LLC (USA): Firebase — Authentication, Cloud Firestore (stored in the USA), Analytics, Crashlytics - Items: the account, cloud save, app statistics and crash reports in section 2 f. Unity Technologies SF (USA) / Unity Technologies ApS (Denmark): Unity Ads, the second ad network - Items: as in section 2(d) — under Unity's privacy policy (unity.com/legal/privacy-policy) If you do not want this, you can play offline against the computer (no online matches), choose non-personalized ads where asked, and not make purchases.

6. Your Rights

You may ask at any time to access, correct, delete or stop processing your information. - Wipe your progress: Settings > Reset Data - Change your ad choices (where the law asks): Settings > Ad privacy - Delete online / statistics data or anything else: email us (see the data deletion page) and we will act without delay. - Delete your account and its cloud save at once: Settings > Account > Delete account

7. Children

The Service does not collect age information and is not directed at children under 13. Children under 14 should make purchases only with a legal guardian's consent. A guardian may ask us to access or delete a child's information.

8. Security

We use encrypted transmission (HTTPS / WSS), keep only the account identifiers sign-in needs, protect the cloud save so that only its owner can read it, and limit administrator access.

9. Automatic Collection

The Service itself sets no cookies and uses no advertising identifier for its own statistics. The ads (Google AdMob, Unity Ads) may use the advertising identifier and similar technologies as described in section 2(d). Google Analytics for Firebase uses an app instance ID, not the advertising identifier.

10. Privacy Officer

MK Game Studio Email: redhat9p0@gmail.com

11. Changes to This Policy

We will announce changes in the app or on this page at least 7 days before they take effect. Effective date: November 14, 2026

1. 총칙

엠케이 게임 스튜디오(MK Game Studio)(이하 '운영자')는 모바일 게임 'Cannonfall'(이하 '서비스')을 개인정보 보호법 등 관련 법령에 따라 운영하며, 필요한 최소한의 정보만 처리합니다. 회원가입과 계정은 없으며 게스트로 이용합니다.

2. 처리하는 정보와 목적

가. 기기에만 저장되는 정보 - 게임 진행(도달 웨이브, 보석, 코인, 대포·꾸미기, 강화, 설정), 광고 횟수, 구매 상태: 진행 저장. 운영자는 볼 수 없습니다. 스토어 앱은 계정 아래 클라우드에도 사본을 둡니다(아래 계정 항목). 나. 온라인 대전(온라인으로 플레이할 때) - 기기에서 만든 무작위 플레이어 식별자, 이름 'Guest', 대포 모습과 장비, 대전 데이터(조작, 결과, 랭킹 포인트): 다른 이용자와의 대전 운영 다. 익명 게임 통계(자동) - 무작위 기기 식별자, 세션 식별자, 게임 이벤트(웨이브 시작·종료, 강화, 상점·광고 결과, 오류 등), 기기 종류(휴대폰/태블릿), OS, 앱 버전, 시각: 게임 개선. 이름, 이메일, 광고 식별자, 계정은 포함하지 않습니다. 라. 광고(Google AdMob, Google 동의 도구 UMP; AdMob 광고가 없을 때 Unity Ads) - Google은 IP 주소(대략적 위치), 기기·앱 정보, 광고 상호작용을 수집할 수 있습니다: 광고 게재·측정, 부정 방지. 서비스는 추적 허용(App Tracking Transparency)을 요청하지 않으므로 광고 식별자(IDFA)는 쓰이지 않습니다. - Unity Ads(Unity Technologies)는 AdMob 광고가 없을 때만 나오며, IP 주소(대략적 위치), 공급업체 식별자(IDFV), 기기·앱 정보, 광고 상호작용을 수집할 수 있습니다: 광고 게재·측정, 부정 방지. 추적 허용을 묻지 않으므로 광고 식별자(IDFA)는 쓰이지 않습니다. 법이 동의를 요구하는 지역에서 동의하지 않으면 비맞춤 광고만 나옵니다. 마. 구매(RevenueCat) - 익명 RevenueCat 이용자 식별자, 구매한 상품 ID, 구매·복원 내역, App Store 영수증 정보: 구매 확인, 아이템 지급, 복원. 카드 번호 등 결제 정보는 Apple이 처리하며 운영자는 받지 않습니다. 바. 계정과 클라우드 저장(스토어 앱, Firebase Authentication / Cloud Firestore) - 무작위 Firebase 이용자 ID(처음엔 게스트), 계정을 연결하면 Google 또는 Apple 이용자 ID와 이메일 주소(Apple은 중계 주소일 수 있음): 진행 상황을 클라우드에 보관하고 다른 기기에서 이어 하기 - 저장된 게임 진행(가항의 항목): 그 이용자 ID 아래 보관하며 본인만 읽을 수 있습니다. 사. 앱 통계와 오류 보고(스토어 앱, Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics) - 앱 인스턴스 ID, 다항의 게임 이벤트, 기기 모델, OS, 앱 버전, 국가·지역(IP 주소로 추정), 비정상 종료·오류 보고: 이용 행태 파악과 오류 수정. 광고 식별자는 수집하지 않습니다. 아. 이메일 문의(선택) - 이메일 주소와 문의 내용: 답변

Google(광고)과 RevenueCat이 보유한 데이터는 각 사의 보관 정책을 따릅니다. 사. 계정과 클라우드 저장: 계정을 삭제할 때까지(설정 > 계정 > 계정 삭제), 삭제 즉시 파기. 앱 통계(Firebase Analytics): 14개월. 오류 보고: 90일.
